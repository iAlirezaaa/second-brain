# Second Brain OS — founding idea index v1 (frozen 2026-11-03)

The attached archival source snapshots are the full historical primary sources. This index makes their distinctive ideas easy to find without replacing or rewriting those originals.

1. **Human and agent together:** a knowledge OS that is usable by humans browsing files and by agents executing bounded work; both have access to legible, cited, organized knowledge.
2. **Classic second brain:** capture URLs, addresses, books, films watched/to watch, quotes, conversations, ideas, plans and experiences; recover them by topic, context and source.
3. **Readable, strongly organized repository:** stable numbered zones, meaningful files and root navigation; the repository itself must work as a knowledge base, not an opaque database or unstructured warehouse.
4. **Independent and portable:** Markdown and standard records as canonical data; no compulsory Obsidian, provider, model or cloud; interface/engine adapters replaceable.
5. **Identity-aware memory:** episodic, semantic, project, procedural and agent lessons with provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, expiration and human control.
6. **Associative/ADHD intelligence:** surface useful weak connections between distant areas while labeling hypotheses and preserving citations.
7. **Accountable multi-agent OS:** identities, scoped permissions, skill discovery, task-first sessions, handoff, independent criticism and reversible execution.
8. **Improvement without self-deception:** measurable quality, benchmark comparisons, quarantine, safety gates, owner-approved promotion, records of both failures and successes.
9. **Simulate then change:** Twin World and previews for risky operations; integrity, compatibility, backup and rollback trump novelty.
10. **Maturity Engineering:** independently evolve storage, retrieval, backend, compute and language based on workload, quality and cost; remain lightweight until growth actually justifies complexity.

**Identity change rule:** Never remove, silently dilute or replace one of these ideas inside a nightly improvement cycle. Propose FROM → TO with evidence, user impact, alternatives and rollback; wait for explicit owner approval. Historic archive copies stay unchanged.
