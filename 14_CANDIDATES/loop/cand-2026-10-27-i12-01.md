# cand-2026-10-27-i12-01

**Invariant.** the registry used to be create-only (issue #12): a wrong record could only be
"fixed" by filing a second one — the duplicate-truth pattern the repo deletes everywhere else.
And the `supersedes` relation was declared in `15_SYSTEM/config/system.json` with a meaning
nothing implemented: both records stayed in the active index, zero errors, zero warnings.

What this row states, and what the fix implements:

- amendments go through the same door as creation: `edit` writes a plan, nothing is touched
  until `apply --approve`;
- the refused cases above never reach the disk;
- `supersedes` does what its definition says: a superseded record leaves the active index
  (`catalog.json` marks `active: false` with `superseded_by`), `status` and `index` count it
  as retired, and `doctor` names the retirement as a warning instead of staying quiet.

(`tests/test_phase21_registry_enumeration_and_amendments.py`, battery
`t_markdown_and_amendments` in `15_SYSTEM/scripts/test_hades.py`), but the row's own
measurement is a real run: set `probe:` to `15_SYSTEM/scripts/test_hades.py`, flip the
verdict to what that run supports, then
`python scripts/probe.py --candidate 14_CANDIDATES/loop/cand-2026-10-27-i12-01.md`,
`python scripts/candidate_check.py --file 14_CANDIDATES/loop/cand-2026-10-27-i12-01.md`,
